# Happy Christmas 🎄

Build a sparkling Christmas tree with your hands: make gestures in front of your camera (✊ 🖐 ☝️ ✌️ 👋) and the tree grows stage by stage, with Silent Night playing.

Play it: https://ymt3232111-pixel.github.io/xmas-tree/

Hand tracking by Google MediaPipe. Music: Silent Night (Franz Xaver Gruber, 1818, public domain), arranged for piano and violin, rendered with the FluidR3 GM soundfont.
